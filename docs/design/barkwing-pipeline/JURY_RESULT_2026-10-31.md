## Shared Findings

- The elevated body and separate shadow communicate flight.
- The round segmented torso and stick-like legs read as a generic insect.
- Branch-and-leaf anatomy is not strong enough to communicate the Deepwood identity.
- The near wing dominates while the far wing collapses in the review angle.
- Only wing animation exists. Attack, hit, movement, and defeat feedback remain absent.
- The GLB is small and cleanly re-imports, but runtime browser and phone evidence remain missing.

## Next Refinement

Spend the next refinement cycle on silhouette, not surface resolution:

1. Replace the round thorax with an angular twisted-bark torso.
2. Enlarge both wings and preserve their separation in angled and top-down views.
3. Replace straight legs with hooked root talons and visible joints.
4. Add torn layered leaf membranes over strong bark ribs.
5. Capture front, side, top, three-quarter, and small gameplay-size views before rigging.

Do not integrate this draft into runtime. Do not spend Meshy credits on rigging or high-resolution textures until the generated base model passes the silhouette gate.
